🤖 What Is Memora?

Memora is a smart Android app that helps you remember commitments, deadlines, and important details—without you having to type them out. Imagine you're reading an email or a message that says "Call the dentist on Friday at 3 PM" or "Submit the report by Tuesday". Normally, you'd have to switch to another app, create a note, set a reminder... it's tedious.

With Memora, a small floating button sits on top of whatever app you're using. Just tap it once, and Memora instantly "reads" the text on your screen using incredibly fast AI technology (Groq responses in just 0.25 seconds, plus Google's Gemini VLM for visual understanding).

Memora then extracts key information like:

  • 🗓️ Dates and times
  • 📋 Action items and tasks
  • 📝 Any specific commitments you make or receive

It saves all of this automatically to your calendar or notes—so you never forget an important commitment again. No typing. No switching apps. Just one tap.

🔍 How Memora Works (In Plain English)

Memora combines two powerful AI technologies to make capturing commitments effortless:

1️⃣ Groq (Speed)

Groq processes text at lightning speed—about 0.25 seconds. When you tap the button, Memora sends the screen text to Groq for extremely fast analysis, so you get your result nearly instantly.

2️⃣ Gemini VLM (Understanding)

Gemini VLM (Visual Language Model) helps Memora actually see and understand your screen visually. So it can pick out relevant pieces—like a meeting time hidden in a paragraph, or a deadline in a chat message—rather than just grabbing random words.

Together, they capture the meaningful stuff from your screen and save it where you need it: your calendar, your notes, or both.
